# AI Night Shopping Platform - MVP v1

## Goal
Enable a US-based customer to discover and shop products from Hyderabad retailers using an AI-assisted shopping experience.

## Primary Customer Flow
1. Customer visits the site
2. Customer describes what they are looking for
3. AI captures intent, budget, occasion, preferences, and constraints
4. AI recommends matching products
5. Customer compares or saves products
6. Customer requests a live shopping session if needed
7. Retailer receives the request and customer context
8. Customer completes a mock checkout/order

## Primary Demo Scenario
"I need a traditional Indian outfit for my daughter's event next weekend. Budget around $250."

## MVP Features
- Landing page
- AI shopping assistant
- Sample product catalog
- Product recommendations
- Product comparison
- Favorites
- Live shopping request
- Retailer request view
- Mock cart and checkout
